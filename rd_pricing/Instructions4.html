A different earnings table applies depending on your investment decision and your counterpart's investment decision.

There are four possible investment profiles:

  • Both you and your counterpart invest.
  • Neither you nor your counterpart invests.
  • You invest, but your counterpart does not invest.
  • You do not invest, but your counterpart invests.

After both investment decisions have been made, your price-decision screen will display the earnings table that applies to you. The same earnings table will remain in effect during all pricing rounds of the current match.

If only one producer invests, you and your counterpart will see different earnings tables because the two producers have different production costs and investment costs.

How to Read the Earnings Tables

You always read an earnings table from your own point of view.

  • Your price determines which row is used.
  • Your counterpart's price determines which column is used.
  • The number at the intersection of the selected row and column is your earnings for that pricing round.

You and your counterpart can each choose from eight prices, so there are 64 possible price combinations in each earnings table.

Example: Both Producers Invest
