{% block title %} Summary of Part 1 {% endblock %} {% block content %}

In Part 1, you will make decisions as a seller-producer in a series of matches.

  • At the beginning of each match, you will be randomly paired with another participant, who will remain your counterpart throughout that match.
  • You and your counterpart will each make a YES or NO investment decision. If you choose YES, your investment will succeed with an 80% probability and fail with a 20% probability.
  • Each producer's investment outcome is determined independently. The combination of the two investment outcomes determines which earnings table applies during the match.
  • In every pricing round, you and your counterpart will each choose a price simultaneously. Your earnings depend on both price choices and the earnings table that applies.
  • At the end of each pricing round, you will learn your counterpart's price and your earnings for that round.
  • After each pricing round, there is an 80% probability that the match will continue and a 20% probability that it will end.
  • If the match continues, the same investment outcomes and earnings table remain in effect. If the match ends, a new match begins with a newly assigned counterpart and new investment decisions.
  • You will be paid for all pricing rounds completed in Part 1.

You will now take a computerized quiz. You will earn $0.5 for each correct answer.
