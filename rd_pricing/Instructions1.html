This experiment is a study of group and individual decision-making. The amount of money you earn depends partly on the decisions that you make, so you should read the instructions carefully.

The money you earn will be paid privately to you, in cash, at the end of the experiment.

This experiment consists of three parts. You will first receive the instructions for Part 1. After Part 1 is completed, you will receive the instructions for the remaining parts. The instructions for each part explain how your earnings in that part will be determined.

The three parts are independent. Your decisions and earnings in one part will not affect your decisions or earnings in any other part.

Please put away your cell phones and other distracting devices for the duration of the experiment.

If you have any questions, please raise your hand.
