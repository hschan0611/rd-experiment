At the beginning of each match, you and your counterpart simultaneously make a YES or NO investment decision. You will learn your counterpart's decision only after both of you have made your decisions.

Investment decisions affect production costs and earnings opportunities in the subsequent pricing stage. The investment decisions made at the beginning of the match apply to all pricing rounds in that match. New investment decisions are made only when a new match begins.

Earnings Tables

The earnings table that applies to you depends on your investment decision and your counterpart's investment decision. There are four possible combinations:

  • Both you and your counterpart invest.
  • Neither you nor your counterpart invests.
  • You invest, but your counterpart does not invest.
  • You do not invest, but your counterpart invests.

If only one producer invests, you and your counterpart will see different earnings tables because you have different production costs and investment costs. The applicable earnings table remains the same for all pricing rounds in the match.

How to Read the Earnings Tables

You and your counterpart can each choose from eight price choices, numbered 1 through 8. You always read the earnings table from your own point of view:

  • Your price choice determines the row.
  • Your counterpart's price choice determines the column.
  • The number at the intersection of the row and column is your earnings for that pricing round.

Because each producer has eight price choices, each earnings table contains 64 possible combinations of price choices.
