In Part 2, you will be randomly paired with another person, whom we will refer to as the other person.

You will not know who the other person is, and the other person will not be informed about your identity.

You will make a series of decisions about allocating points between yourself and the other person.

For each question, select the allocation that you prefer most by selecting one button in the middle row.

You may select only one allocation for each question. There are no right or wrong answers. These choices concern your personal preferences.

After you have made all your choices, one of the allocation choices will be randomly selected by the software. For this choice, the software will randomly assign one person from your group (you or the other) the role of Receiver and the other the role of the Sender. The allocation choice made by the Sender will be enforced. This allocation will be paid in cash to both the Sender and the Receiver.

Points will be converted into dollars at a rate of 1 point = $0.05.

Example

In the example below, a person chose the allocation that gives 50 points to herself and 40 points to the other person.

In dollar terms, this allocation gives 50 × $0.05 = $2.50 to the person making the choice and 40 × $0.05 = $2.00 to the other person.
